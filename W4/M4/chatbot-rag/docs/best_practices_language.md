# CV Language — Strong vs. Weak Phrases

## Why Language Matters
Recruiters spend 6–10 seconds on an initial CV scan. Weak language blends in. Strong language stands out and signals ownership, impact, and capability.

## Weak Phrases to Avoid

| Weak | Why It's Weak |
|------|--------------|
| Responsible for... | Describes a duty, not an outcome |
| Helped with... | Vague, no ownership |
| Assisted in... | Passive, supporting role implied |
| Worked on... | No direction or result |
| Involved in... | Non-committal |
| Tried to... | Signals failure or uncertainty |
| Was part of a team that... | Buries individual contribution |
| Hard worker | Everyone says this, it means nothing |
| Team player | Cliché with no evidence |
| Passionate about... | Show it through achievements, don't state it |
| Fast learner | Prove it with certifications or examples |
| Detail-oriented | Prove it through error-free, clean writing |

## Strong Action Verbs by Category

### Leadership
Led, Managed, Directed, Oversaw, Mentored, Coached, Supervised, Mobilized, Championed

### Building / Creating
Built, Developed, Designed, Architected, Launched, Established, Founded, Implemented, Deployed

### Improving
Optimized, Streamlined, Reduced, Eliminated, Improved, Restructured, Transformed, Modernized, Accelerated

### Achieving / Delivering
Delivered, Achieved, Exceeded, Completed, Secured, Generated, Drove, Produced, Executed

### Analyzing
Analyzed, Evaluated, Researched, Identified, Assessed, Audited, Forecasted, Modeled, Mapped

### Communicating
Presented, Negotiated, Influenced, Collaborated, Liaised, Authored, Facilitated, Advised

## Quantification Examples

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| Improved website speed | Reduced page load time by 45% through image compression and caching |
| Managed a team | Led a cross-functional team of 7 to deliver a $2M platform on schedule |
| Increased sales | Grew quarterly revenue by 28% through targeted outreach to 150 SME accounts |
| Reduced costs | Cut infrastructure costs by $40K annually by migrating to serverless architecture |
| Helped onboard users | Onboarded 1,200 enterprise users in 6 weeks with a 94% satisfaction score |

## The Achievement Formula
**Action verb + what you did + measurable result**

Example:
- Bad: "Responsible for customer support"
- Good: "Resolved 95% of customer tickets within 24 hours, achieving a 4.8/5 satisfaction score across 3,000 monthly interactions"

## Red Flag Phrases in Saudi/Gulf CVs Specifically
- "Seeking a suitable position" — says nothing
- "I am a hard-working individual" — use "I" sparingly or not at all
- "References available upon request" — outdated, remove it
- "Date of availability: immediately" — only include if specifically asked
- Listing hobbies like "reading" or "traveling" without context — wastes space
