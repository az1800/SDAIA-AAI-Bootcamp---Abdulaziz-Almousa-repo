# CV Conventions — United States

## Resume vs. CV
- In the US, the document is called a "resume", not a "CV"
- A CV in the US context means an academic document (multi-page, for professors and researchers)
- For most jobs, a 1-page resume is standard; 2 pages for 10+ years of experience

## What to NEVER Include (Legal Reasons)
- Photo — employers cannot legally consider appearance in hiring
- Date of birth or age
- Marital status
- Race, ethnicity, or nationality
- Religion
- Gender (beyond your name)
- Social Security Number
Including any of the above raises red flags and may disqualify you

## Contact Information
- Name, phone, professional email, LinkedIn, city and state (no full address needed)
- GitHub or portfolio link for tech roles

## Summary vs. Objective
- "Objective" statements are outdated — replace with a 2–3 line professional summary
- Tailor the summary to each application

## Work Experience
- Quantify everything: numbers, percentages, dollar amounts, team sizes
- Use the STAR or XYZ format: "Accomplished X by doing Y, resulting in Z"
- Action verbs are critical: Spearheaded, Optimized, Delivered, Generated, Scaled
- Gaps in employment should be briefly addressed in the cover letter, not the resume

## ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)
- Most US companies use ATS to filter resumes before a human sees them
- Use keywords directly from the job description
- Avoid tables, columns, headers/footers, and graphics — ATS cannot parse them
- Use standard section titles: "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills"
- Submit as Word (.docx) or plain PDF depending on instructions

## Education
- Listed after experience (unless you are a new graduate)
- GPA: include only if 3.5+ and you graduated within the last 3 years
- Relevant coursework and honors are optional for new grads

## Skills
- Keep it relevant and honest
- For tech roles: list languages, frameworks, tools, cloud platforms
- Do not list Microsoft Office unless it is specifically relevant

## Cover Letter
- Expected for most professional roles
- Should complement, not repeat, the resume
- 3 paragraphs: why this role, what you bring, closing call to action

## Formatting
- Clean, minimal design
- Standard fonts: Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman
- Margins: 0.5 to 1 inch
- No photos, no colors (or very minimal), no icons
- File name: FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf
