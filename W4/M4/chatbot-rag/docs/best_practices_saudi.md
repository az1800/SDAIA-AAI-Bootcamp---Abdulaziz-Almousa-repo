# CV Conventions — Saudi Arabia

## General Context
Saudi Arabia has a mix of public sector (government), semi-government (Saudi Aramco, STC, SABIC), and private sector employers. Expectations vary across these sectors but some norms are consistent.

## What is Typically Expected
- Full name in both Arabic and English
- Nationality is commonly included (especially for Saudis due to Saudization/Vision 2030 hiring preferences)
- National ID number is sometimes requested on application forms but should NOT be on the CV itself
- A professional photo is still common in Saudi CVs, though it is becoming less required in international-facing companies
- Date of birth is still commonly included, though modern practice is moving away from it

## Saudization and Vision 2030
- If you are Saudi, make your nationality visible — it is an advantage in most local hiring
- Highlight any alignment with Vision 2030 sectors: tourism, entertainment, fintech, logistics, healthcare
- Government and semi-government roles value: certifications, training programs (e.g. HRDF, Misk, SDAIA), and community involvement
- Tamheer and Cooperative Training internships should be listed — they are well recognized

## Language
- Bilingual CVs (Arabic + English) are common for government roles
- For private sector and international companies operating in KSA, English-only is preferred
- If submitting in Arabic, ensure formal Arabic (فصحى) is used — not colloquial

## Education
- Saudi universities are well recognized locally: KAUST, KSU, KFUPM, KAU, UQU
- Mention scholarship programs if applicable: King Abdullah Scholarship, Ministry of Education sponsorship
- Overseas degrees (UK, US, Canada) carry strong weight — mention the country

## Experience
- Include Saudi-specific context where relevant: ZATCA compliance, VAT implementation, NCA cybersecurity frameworks, Vision 2030 projects
- Mention any experience with government platforms: Qiwa, Absher, Muqeem, Etimad
- Military service or national service, if completed, can be listed briefly

## Certifications
- Saudi-specific: SOCPA (accounting), TVTC vocational certs, NCA certifications
- International certs are highly valued: PMP, CPA, CFA, AWS, Google, Microsoft
- HRDF-funded training and certifications should be mentioned

## What to Avoid
- Do not include tribal or family affiliations
- Do not include mosque or religious group memberships
- Avoid listing wasta (connections) — it is understood but never written
- Do not use informal photos — formal attire only if photo is included

## Formatting Preferences
- Clean and formal — ornate design is not favored in corporate settings
- Arabic CVs: right-to-left layout, formal headers
- Length: 1–2 pages standard; government applications sometimes accept longer
